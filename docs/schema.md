## Notes (hand-written)

Migration 016 adds `tb_almacen.unidad_medida` (default `unidad`) and `tb_ventas.tipo_documento` (default `boleta`). Sale document types are internal classifications, not SII DTEs.

It also adds `tb_documentos_comerciales`, `tb_documento_comercial_items`, and `tb_documentos_comerciales_historial` for multi-item quotations and purchase orders. These documents do not change stock; corrections retain before-and-after snapshots with the acting user and timestamp. Quantities remain integers in this version.

Migration 017 adds `tb_movimientos_inventario`, an append-only stock ledger. New purchases, sales, returns, adjustments, and purchase corrections add signed quantity entries without rewriting prior entries. The migration imports movements available in existing tables and estimates a missing opening balance from current stock; earlier, unaudited corrections cannot be reconstructed.
